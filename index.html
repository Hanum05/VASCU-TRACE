08 / RESEARCH FRAMEWORK

About the VASCU-TRACE concept

Research direction: multimodal peripheral signal acquisition and exploratory vascular profiling in a primary-care research context.

Research background

Blood pressure and vascular health assessment are important in primary care. Optical PPG and bioimpedance spectroscopy provide different, potentially complementary signal domains. Their integration is a research hypothesis that requires systematic engineering and clinical evaluation.

Research objectives

Explore sensor acquisition principles, characterize signal features, design a transparent multimodal processing pipeline, and develop a testable framework for future validation.

Technological innovation

Dual-mode sensing combines optical pulse-related changes with frequency-dependent electrical tissue properties in a shared signal-processing workflow.

Proposed workflow

Signal acquisition
Optical PPG and BIS channels are acquired as separate synthetic streams with distinct physical principles.

Expected contribution

A reproducible research demonstrator for exploring multimodal sensing, feature extraction, and transparent data visualization.

Research limitations

Signal artifacts, inter-individual variation, calibration, reference-standard agreement, and clinical generalizability remain unresolved.

Future development

Bench testing, sensor characterization, protocol design, reference-device comparison, ethics review, and prospective validation.

Research disclaimer. VASCU-TRACE is a conceptual biomedical engineering demonstrator. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose arteriosclerosis or atherosclerosis, and does not directly measure or clinically validate central aortic pressure. All displayed waveforms, numbers, classifications, and reports are synthetic demonstrations. Clinical use would require appropriate regulatory, technical, and clinical validation.
